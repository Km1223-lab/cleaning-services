We ask that you let us know as early as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule a booking, ideally at least 24 hours in advance, so we can reassign the crew's time. Repeated late cancellations may affect our ability to offer short-notice bookings in future.

6. Payment

Payment terms and accepted methods will be confirmed with you directly by our team at the time of booking or on completion of the job.

We aim to complete every job to the standard agreed with you. If any part of the work isn't up to standard, please raise it with our crew during the final walkthrough, or contact us as soon as possible afterward, so we can make it right.
