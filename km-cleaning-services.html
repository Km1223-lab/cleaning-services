What we do

A service for every kind of mess

From a weekly tidy-up to a full post-construction clear-out, our crews come equipped, trained, and ready to work off a checklist — not a guess.

Residential cleaning

Routine or one-off cleaning for houses and apartments — kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, done to a standard you can set your watch by.

Office & commercial cleaning

Daily, weekly or after-hours cleaning for offices, retail spaces and business premises, with a crew that respects your working hours.

Deep cleaning

The kind of clean that gets behind appliances, inside cabinets and along skirting boards — ideal for a seasonal reset.

Post-construction cleaning

Dust, debris and paint splatter removal so a newly built or renovated space is ready to move into, not just swept.

Move-in / move-out cleaning

A thorough clean timed around your move, so you hand over — or move into — a space that's genuinely spotless.

Carpet, sofa & upholstery care

Steam and shampoo cleaning for carpets, sofas and curtains that lifts stains and odours, not just surface dust.

Fumigation & pest control

Safe, targeted treatment for common household pests, carried out by trained technicians using approved products.

Window & exterior cleaning

Streak-free windows, balconies and exterior surfaces, including hard-to-reach spots in multi-storey buildings.

Event & after-party cleaning

Fast turnaround cleaning after weddings, parties and corporate events, so the venue is handed back in top condition.

How it works

Four steps to a cleaner space

No call-centre runaround. You speak to a real coordinator from the first message to the final walkthrough.

01

Request a quote

Tell us the space, the service and a convenient date. We reply same day with a clear, itemised quote.

02

We schedule & prep

We assign a vetted crew, confirm the checklist for your job type, and lock in the time slot with you.

03

Our team cleans

The crew arrives in KM uniform with their own equipment and works through the agreed checklist, room by room.

04

Walkthrough & feedback

We walk the space with you before we leave. Anything not up to standard gets fixed on the spot.
