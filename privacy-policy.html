Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

KM Cleaning Services ("we", "us", "our") provides residential, commercial and specialist cleaning services in Nairobi, Kenya. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website (www.kmcleaningservice.uk) and our AI booking assistant, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

When you request a quote, use our AI booking assistant, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • The service you're interested in
  • Your location or estate (e.g. "Kilimani, Nairobi")
  • Your preferred date for the service
  • Any additional details you choose to share about the job

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and our services are intended for adults arranging cleaning for a home or business.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and arrange a booking
  • To contact you by phone or WhatsApp about your request
  • To schedule and coordinate the cleaning crew assigned to your job
  • To keep basic records of past and upcoming jobs for our own operations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Our AI booking assistant

Our website includes a chat assistant, available in English and Kiswahili, that helps you put together a booking request. Messages you send it are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI model to generate helpful responses. Once the assistant has gathered your service, location, date, name and phone number, this information is stored so our team can follow up with you, and a summary is prepared for you to send to us on WhatsApp.

4. How long we keep information

We retain booking request details for as long as reasonably needed to deliver our services and maintain basic business records, after which we delete or anonymise it. If you'd like your information removed sooner, contact us using the details below.

5. WhatsApp

If you contact us or send a request via WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to this one, as WhatsApp is operated by Meta, a separate company from KM Cleaning Services.

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information from our records

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services or our tools change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent version.
